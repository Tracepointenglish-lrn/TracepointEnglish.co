A1 · BEGINNER

Present Simple

Use the Present Simple for habits, routines, facts, and things that are generally true.

1. The basic pattern

Subject + base verbI work every day.
He / She / It + verb-sShe works every day.

2. Negative sentences

Use do not (don't) or does not (doesn't) + base verb.

I don't drink coffee.

He doesn't drink coffee.

In negative sentences, the main verb returns to its base form: doesn't work (not doesn't works).

3. Questions

Do / Does + subject + base verb?Does she speak English?
Catatan Indonesia

Gunakan does untuk he, she, it. Setelah does/doesn't, kata kerja tidak memakai -s lagi.
